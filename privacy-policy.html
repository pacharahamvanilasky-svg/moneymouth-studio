Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Moneymouth Studio ("the App") is a desktop publishing tool that lets a creator upload their own finished videos to their own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. This policy explains what information the App uses and how.

Information the App accesses

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives, with your permission:

  • Your TikTok open ID, display name, username and avatar, so that the App can show which account a video will be posted to.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, so that the App can upload videos you choose to post.
  • The privacy and interaction settings TikTok returns for your account (for example, which privacy levels you can choose), so that the App only offers options your account allows.
  • Basic statistics of videos you posted (views, likes, comments, shares), only if you grant the video.list permission.

Information you provide

  • The video files, captions and post settings you select in the App. Videos are read from your own computer and sent only to TikTok.

How the information is used

  • To upload and publish videos to your TikTok account, or send them to your TikTok inbox as drafts, only when you press the post button.
  • To show the posting status and the link to your post.
  • To record the performance of your own posts for your own reference.

Storage and security

  • Tokens and settings are stored only on the computer where the App runs, in a file readable only by your user account. The App has no server and does not send your data to the App's developer or to any third party other than TikTok.
  • Access tokens are sent to TikTok only in request headers over HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Information is sent only to TikTok, as needed to perform the actions you request.

Retention and deletion

  • You can disconnect the App at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. This revokes the App's access immediately.
  • You can delete the local credentials file at any time to remove all stored tokens.
  • Post logs remain on your own computer until you delete them.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
